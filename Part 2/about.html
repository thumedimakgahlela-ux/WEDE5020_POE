Background of Ubuntu Rise Foundation

Ubuntu Rise Foundation is a non-profit organisation established with the vision of uplifting communities and improving the quality of life for vulnerable individuals and families. Inspired by the African philosophy of Ubuntu, "I am because we are", the foundation believes that lasting change is achieved when people work together to support one another.

Together, we are making a difference, one community at a time.

About us

The organisation was created to address social challenges such as poverty, unemployment, limited access to education, and community development. Through partnerships, volunteerism, skills development programmes, educational support, and community outreach initiatives, Ubuntu Rise Foundation aims to empower people with the knowledge, resources, and opportunities they need to become self-reliant.

Together, they hold a simple message of hope for a brighter future.

What we aim to achieve

Our commitment is to build stronger, more inclusive communities where everyone has the opportunity to grow, succeed, and contribute positively to society. By promoting compassion, dignity, and collaboration, Ubuntu Rise Foundation strives to create lasting social impact and inspire hope for future generations.

Small hands, big dreams, and endless hope.
