## Organisation overview
The group focuses on improving people’s lives through community development, drawing inspiration from the African principle of ubuntu, which means “I am because we are.”
## Website goals and objectives
Goals
•	Raise awareness about the organisation’s mission and community projects.
•	Increase online donations to support programmes.
•	Recruit volunteers and encourage community participation.
•	Allow beneficiaries to apply for assistance online.
•	Share success stories, upcoming events, and news.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
•	Achieve at least 2,000 website visitors per month within the first six months.
•	Receive 100 online donation enquires per month.
•	Recruit 50 new volunteers within six months.
•	Increase newsletter subscriptions by 20% each quarter.
•	Maintain an average website loading time of under 3 seconds.
## Proposed website features and functionality
The website will include:
•	Homepage – Introduction to the organisation and latest news.
•	About us – Organisation history, mission, vision and values.
•	Our programmes – Education, food relief, youth development and environmental projects.
•	Volunteer Page – Volunteer registration form.
•	Donate page – Secure online donations.
•	Events page – Upcoming fundraising events and community activities.
•	Gallery – Photos and videos
•	Success stories – testimonials from beneficiaries.
•	Blog/News – Latest updates.
•	Contact us – Contact form, email, phone number and location map.
Functionality
•	Mobile – friendly responsive design.
•	Secure donation system.
•	Online volunteer registration.
•	Newsletter subscription.
•	Search function.
•	Social media integration.
•	Google maps integration.
•	Contact forms with email notifications
